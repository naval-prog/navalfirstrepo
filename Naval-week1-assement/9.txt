Async/Await Analogy (3 sentences)

Imagine you order food at a restaurant. In synchronous code, you stand at the counter and wait until your food is ready before doing anything else. With async/await, you place the order, do other work while the food is being prepared, and come back when it is ready.

Synchronous Function
import requests

def fetch(url):
    r = requests.get(url, timeout=10)
    r.raise_for_status()
    return r.json()


Async Version Using httpx.AsyncClient
import httpx

async def fetch(url):
    async with httpx.AsyncClient(timeout=10) as client:
        r = await client.get(url)
        r.raise_for_status()
        return r.json()
