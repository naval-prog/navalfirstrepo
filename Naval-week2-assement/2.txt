(a) Embedding:
An embedding is a numerical vector representation of text, images, or data that captures its meaning and relationships.

(b) Vector similarity (and the metric most commonly used):
Vector similarity measures how semantically similar two embeddings are, most commonly using cosine similarity.

(c) Chunk size:
Chunk size is the amount of text split into one piece before creating embeddings and storing it for retrieval.

(d) Chunk overlap:
Chunk overlap is the repeated portion of text shared between consecutive chunks to preserve context across boundaries.

(e) Top-k retrieval:
Top-k retrieval is the process of returning the k most similar or relevant chunks from a vector database for a query.
