With chunk_size = 2000 and chunk_overlap = 0 for a 200-page policy manual, these three retrieval problems are likely:

1. Chunks become too large and contain mixed topics

A 2000-token chunk may include several policies or sections together, making retrieval less precise because the embedding represents many ideas at once instead of one focused topic.

2. Important information may be missed at boundaries

With 0 overlap, if an answer spans the end of one chunk and the beginning of another, retrieval may miss part of the context or return incomplete information.

3. Lower relevance in top-k retrieval

Large chunks often cause the vector search to retrieve broad, partially relevant sections instead of the exact 1–2 paragraph answer the user needs, reducing answer quality.

Better values
chunk_size = 400–800
chunk_overlap = 50–100

Justification:
Since most answers fit within 1–2 paragraphs, smaller chunks with some overlap preserve context while keeping each chunk focused, leading to more accurate and relevant retrieval.
